## Usage

Use the bio clock at the top of a results screen to sum up biological age on one dial. It matches the age clock in the PREDICT app design.

- **Hand** — Points at `bioAge` on the scale, which runs clockwise from the bottom left (`range[0]`, 20 by default) to the bottom right (`range[1]`, 80).
- **Ages** — Biological age, rounded, sits left of the centre; calendar age sits right of it.
- **Badge** — The gap between the two ages, such as `−6 years` or `+4 years`. Pass `yearsValue` to write your own.
- **Readout** — The large number under the dial. It shows `bioAge` to one decimal unless you pass `value`.

The arcs use `--primary` and the hand, ticks and numbers use the text colour, so the clock follows the theme.

Import it from `@workspace/ui/predict/bio-clock`.

```tsx
import { BioClock } from "@workspace/ui/predict/bio-clock"
```

```tsx
<BioClock bioAge={41.2} chronologicalAge={45} />
```

Pair it with a plain-language line and the ageing pace, as the app does:

```tsx
<div className="flex flex-col items-center gap-4">
  <BioClock bioAge={40.4} chronologicalAge={44} />
  <p className="text-center text-base text-foreground">3.6 years younger than your calendar age</p>
  <span className="rounded-full bg-secondary px-3.5 py-1.5 text-xs tabular-nums">0.87× ageing pace</span>
</div>
```

### Notes

- The clock is square, 290 pixels by default (its size in the app design). It never grows wider than its container.
- The hand stops at the ends of `range`. Widen the range for very young or old ages.
- The hand sweeps in on first render. With reduced motion turned on, it starts in place.
- The dial has `role="img"` and an `aria-label` that reads out both ages and the badge.
- Round ages before you pass them if you need a specific precision. The readout shows one decimal.

## Props

Unlisted props pass through to the wrapping `div`, which also takes a `ref`.

| Prop | Type | Default | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bioAge` | `number` | `37.6` | Biological age. The hand points at it, and it is the readout. |
| `chronologicalAge` | `number` | `44` | Calendar age, shown right of the centre. |
| `yearsValue` | `string` | the gap, such as `−6 years` | Badge text. |
| `value` | `ReactNode` | `bioAge` to one decimal | The large readout under the dial. |
| `range` | `[number, number]` | `[20, 80]` | Ages at the start and end of the scale. |
| `size` | `"sm", "default", "lg", "xl", or number` | `default` | Width and height in pixels. |
| `className` | `string` | — | Classes merged onto the wrapper. |

`BioClockProps` is the type of the props. `ClockGaugeProps` is kept as an alias for older imports. The `domains`, `severity`, `segments`, and `currentValue` props from the earlier gauge are gone.
